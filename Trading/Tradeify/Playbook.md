# 📋 Eval Playbook — Tradeify 50k Select

**Account:** $3,000 target · $2,000 EOD drawdown · 3-day min · 40% consistency (eval) · no time limit · **MNQ, AM session**

> **PRIME DIRECTIVE:** The eval has no clock. Survive the day and the target comes to you.

---

## 1. Size — always exactly $200 risk
- **1R = $200.** MNQ = $2/point. `Contracts = floor(200 ÷ (stop_points × 2))`
  - 20-pt stop → 5 contracts · 25-pt → 4 · 40-pt → 2
- **Recompute every trade** so risk is $200 whether the stop is tight or wide. Never round up "for a good feeling."

## 2. Setup — A+ only
- Only the setup I'd screenshot and be proud of (with-trend opening drive / ORB break with confirmation).
- **B setups are how I donate.** Not sure it's A+? It doesn't exist.

## 3. Take profit — the bracket
- Enter → **OCO bracket: −1R stop / +2R target** (or a structure target ≈2R: prior-day H/L, opening-range extension, key level) → **walk away.**
- **One** allowed management move: price tags +1R then stalls hard against me before target → may move stop to breakeven, once, irreversible. Otherwise hands off.
- **No trailing. No "let me see." No moving the target.** The bracket is my anti-tilt armor — I trade distracted from work, so the trade must manage itself.

## 4. Daily limits — my ONLY brake (no firm daily loss limit)
- **Daily goal +2R (+$400):** hit it → DONE. Platform off. Green locked.
- **Daily stop −2R (−$400):** hit it → DONE. Screen off. No "one more."
- **Max 2 trades/day.** After two A+ trades, done regardless of result.
- Every day ends one of three ways: **+$400, −$400, or 2 trades taken** — whichever first. The day is *always* small.

## 5. Finish-line protocol — where I die
- Within **one good day (~$400–500) of the $3,000 target:** cut to **half size ($100), ONE trade/day.**
- Taking **3–4 extra days to finish is correct, not weak.** Drawdown line is tightest and ego loudest right here.
- Mantra: **"Near the target, I trade smaller and slower — never bigger and faster."**

## 6. Environment rules — from the Friday blow-up
- **Slammed at work? Don't trade.** Can't give the first 45 min real attention → flat. Skipped day = $0. Distracted day = the account.
- **No Friday finish-forcing.** Target doesn't expire over the weekend.
- **One-and-done preferred:** trade 1 hits +2R → strongly consider stopping before trade 2.

## 7. Scoreboard
- **Week 1, the only metric is Rule-Follow %, not money.**
- Follow rules + lose = **a WIN.** Break rules + win = **a LOSS** (a time bomb).

---

### Pass math
2 trades/day at my real edge ≈ +1.3–1.6R/day → **$3k in ~10–12 trading days.** Worst case +0.5R/day → ~30 days. With −$400 stop + 10R room, **blowing up requires breaking this sheet.** Follow it and the only variable left is time — and time is free.
